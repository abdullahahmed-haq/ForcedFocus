#### Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment**: The interface looks distinctly AI-generated. The abundance of purple/indigo gradients, "Inter" font, overused em-dashes, nested and repeated card templates, and gray text on colored backgrounds strongly match the AI aesthetic of 2026. The layout is somewhat cluttered with identical card grids (Blacklist, Whitelist, Permanent Block) that could be simplified.

**Deterministic scan**: The CLI detector found 19 issues. Most critically:
- `ai-color-palette` (Purple/violet gradients and cyan-on-dark) in `settings.html`.
- `gradient-text` used for headings/metrics in `styles.css`.
- `overused-font` ("Inter") across multiple files.
- `gray-on-color` text (gray text on indigo background) resulting in poor contrast.
- `layout-transition` (animating width) causing layout thrash.
- `em-dash-overuse` (12+ em-dashes) in body copy.

#### Overall Impression
The app has a solid structural foundation with useful functional controls, but the visual aesthetic is generic and distinctly AI-generated. The UI feels like a template due to the overused typography, gradient text, and repetitive card layouts.

#### What's Working
- **Clear Navigation**: The main tab-based navigation (Dashboard, Rules, Schedules, Tracking) makes logical sense.
- **Robust Controls**: The functionality (Pomodoro vs Standard, Smart Templates) is comprehensive and clear.

#### Priority Issues

- **[P1] AI Aesthetic Overload**
  - **Why it matters**: Purple gradients, gradient text, and gray-on-color text make the app feel generic and reduce legibility.
  - **Fix**: Remove gradient text. Use a solid, distinctive brand color. Fix the gray-on-indigo text to white for contrast.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable colorize`

- **[P1] Repetitive Card Structure**
  - **Why it matters**: Endless identical cards (Domain Groups, Permanent Block, Blacklist, Whitelist) cause visual fatigue.
  - **Fix**: Break out of identical cards. Use lists with subtle dividers or inline panels for the domain rules.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable layout`

- **[P2] Overused Typography**
  - **Why it matters**: "Inter" makes the site look like every other tool. A flat type hierarchy makes scanning difficult.
  - **Fix**: Switch out Inter for a more distinctive sans-serif or geometric font, and increase size contrast between headings and body text.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable typeset`

- **[P2] Em-Dash Overuse**
  - **Why it matters**: It's a syntactic AI tell that affects the voice and tone of the copy.
  - **Fix**: Rewrite the copy in index.html to use commas, colons, or standard punctuation instead of em-dashes.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable clarify`

#### Persona Red Flags

**Alex (Power User)**
- No apparent keyboard shortcuts for switching tabs or toggling focus modes.
- Entering domains requires clicking the small '+' button rather than just hitting Enter or bulk-pasting easily (though pasting multiple is suggested, the interaction might be clunky).

**Sam (Accessibility-Dependent User)**
- The gray text on indigo background (`text-gray-100 on bg-indigo-500`) fails contrast ratio checks, making it hard to read.
- Using `div` and visual badges without clear ARIA live regions for some dynamic status updates (like the timer blob) might leave screen readers uninformed.

#### Minor Observations
- "Rescue Throne" is an interesting concept but the emoji usage (🛡️) combined with gradient text feels slightly game-like rather than productivity-focused.

#### Questions to Consider
- Does the domain management (Blacklist/Whitelist) need to be separated into 3 distinct cards, or could it be one unified table with a type toggle?
- Can we strip out the glassmorphism and gradient text to make it feel more "native" or "pro" rather than a web template?
